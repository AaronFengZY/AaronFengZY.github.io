Research Interests

Embodied intelligence through complementary advances in systems, models, and evaluation.

  • Agent harnesses: task-relevant context organization, grounded reasoning, planning, and executable action generation.
  • Foundation models: multimodal and vision-language-action learning, scalable pretraining and post-training, spatial reasoning, and generalization.
  • Benchmarks and evaluation: assessing multimodal understanding, spatial reasoning, and embodied capabilities to identify gaps and guide improvements.
